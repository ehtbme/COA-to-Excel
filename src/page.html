    What goes into the Excel sheet?

    One row per COA. Highlighted columns are filled from the COA (NA when that COA doesn't state it); the rest are left blank for you to fill in.

      Expiry dates are written as DDMMMYYYY, e.g. 09JUN2029. If the COA gives only month and year, the day is set to 01.

      Everything runs inside this one page: no internet needed, nothing is installed or uploaded.
